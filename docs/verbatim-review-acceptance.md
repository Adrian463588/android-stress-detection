# Verbatim+ — implementation and manual acceptance

Date: 2026-10-06. Target branch: `android-devsecops-20260808`. PRD/AGENTS/DESIGN remain normative; the two pasted references are design input. Physical-device testing is owned by the user. No ADB commands, recording, Watch acquisition, screenshots, or accuracy measurements were executed for this change.

## Delivered behavior

- Indonesian and English retain the provisioned local Sherpa recognizers. Native diarization now uses session-persistent speaker embedding anchors instead of treating per-window cluster numbers as stable identities. Actual mixed-speaker turns are re-decoded from their observed PCM crops. Partial, overlapping, insufficient, ambiguous, and unsupported additional-speaker evidence stays `Unknown`. The current interview contract supports two resolved speakers; roles are manually assigned, not inferred from turn order.
- Final text is analyzed by a versioned, inspectable Indonesian/English lexicon. Hedging, explicit uncertainty, filled pauses, literal repetitions, and self-repairs carry exact character spans, matched text, rule, explanation, source, and utterance range. Whitespace normalization does not remove fillers or negation. Quotation, polite prompts, negation, substring matches, and ordinary Indonesian reduplication have regression tests. These rules are not a validated hesitation or deception classifier.
- Actual openSMILE 3.0.2 SMILEapi runs behind one JNI boundary. Checksum-pinned upstream source is built for `arm64-v8a`, `armeabi-v7a`, and `x86_64`. The same observed PCM supplies eGeMAPSv02 LLD means: voiced pitch, loudness, jitter, shimmer, and other native descriptors. No second microphone, generated waveform, remote inference, or fake feature values are used in production. This is not the full 88-functionals vector, a trained arousal classifier, or a speaking-rate measurement.
- Speaker-specific pitch review markers require warm-up, finite voiced evidence, signal quality, persistence, hysteresis, and cooldown. Features are encrypted with the transcript snapshot and restored when the archive is reopened. Old/missing/invalid evidence remains explicitly unavailable. Acoustic measurements are not labeled as physiological stress predictions.
- Watch monitoring is opt-in, default off. Audio-only sessions do not acquire a Watch lease or start PPG inference. Imported historical audio is audio-only because a newly acquired Watch stream cannot establish its historical alignment. Enabled sessions retain the real independent-channel ONNX/SQA/readiness pipeline; missing Watch or invalid PPG does not fabricate stress values or block audio transcription. No model threshold or release readiness was promoted.
- Transcript review is one lazy scroll container. Final archives are not truncated to the live 200-segment tail. Exact lexical highlights have text labels and accessible explanations; cue playback uses the real utterance range and is disabled during recording/finalization. Export actions wrap on expanded layouts and stack on compact screens. Touch targets, theme colors, progress, and explicit errors are retained.
- `Unduh transkrip TXT` uses Android's document picker and UTF-8. It exports chronological current speaker/text edits, literal cues, acoustic/PPG markers, manual notes, provenance, and gap/finalization warnings. Export waits for text or speaker edits to finish and freezes the selected review before opening the picker. Both edits share one persistence boundary. Raw ASR remains immutable. TXT is plaintext only at the destination explicitly chosen by the user; the existing ZIP export is unchanged.
- Finalization now retains live text corrections as well as speaker corrections. Pause/transport discontinuities close ASR utterances instead of joining speech across missing time. Stop cutoff prevents late acoustic frames from entering a finalized session.
- Live collectors subscribe before capture starts. Final utterances cannot regress to partial text in either the capture archive or session reducer. Serialized live publication reads current authoritative state, deduplicates marker IDs, preserves channel identity, and orders events. Buffered live snapshots are rejected during archived review; saved edits remain authoritative. Lexical cues are chronological, final-only observations, including English typographic contractions, not provisional marker predictions.
- Gap provenance distinguishes an unrecorded pause from dropped ASR work whose PCM is still in WAV. Retained PCM is no longer subtracted during playback seek. Audio-relative and PPG session-elapsed origins are explicitly distinguished in TXT; marker playback targets a real linked final utterance, or remains unavailable rather than guessing the offset. Unlinked audio regions can be reviewed with the main playback controls.

## Manual acceptance checklist

Record the device model, Android version, APK checksum, speech-bundle fingerprint, consented audio language, and observed errors. Keep sensitive audio/PPG outside Git. Do not mark the following as passed from source inspection or a successful build.

- [ ] Install the new APK using an update installation that preserves existing app data. Do not clear/uninstall the application merely to make a test pass.
- [ ] Provision/verify the speech bundle through the real setup/import path. Missing/corrupt models must prevent recording with an actionable unavailable state. Then enable airplane mode and disable Wi-Fi/mobile data. No inference endpoint or cloud fallback should be needed.
- [ ] Start an audio-only Indonesian session; leave PPG off. Record at least 300 seconds with two consenting speakers, including separated turns, one short overlap, fillers, and ordinary Indonesian reduplication. Record a separate English session. Check the actual speaker assignments against the audio; ambiguous evidence must not appear confidently labeled.
- [ ] Verify live partial text is replaceable and does not have a final speaker label; finalized turns preserve observed speech. Note omissions of fillers by ASR rather than treating missing cues as proof of fluent speech.
- [ ] Open a session immediately after starting capture; confirm the first observed utterance appears. Observe final-only cue updates, marker episode updates without duplicate rows, and partial → final replacement. Stop while ASR is draining; no post-cutoff marker should enter the saved result. Reopen/edit the just-completed session and wait beyond the UI sampling interval; an old live snapshot must not replace the saved edits.
- [ ] Pause for a known interval, resume, stop, and save. Listen to each cue/marker and confirm its real utterance range. No pause/dropout may be replaced by fabricated speech or padded biosignal samples.
- [ ] Use clear voiced speech long enough for five valid final utterances per speaker. Inspect actual openSMILE features and independently warmed baselines. Short, unvoiced, ambiguous-speaker, and native-failed segments must abstain. A changed pitch does not establish stress or deception.
- [ ] Edit speaker labels and text, reopen the session, export TXT, and inspect UTF-8 characters, chronological rows, current corrections, literal cue explanations, manual notes, and acoustic/PPG legends. Export must not snapshot a pending text save. Cancel the document picker and confirm there is no success notification or write.
- [ ] Import a real local audio file through the document picker. Confirm the same transcription/diarization/cue/prosody path runs and Watch monitoring is disabled for historical audio.
- [ ] Test microphone allow/deny, notification permission behavior, background recording, screen off, active back → Stop/Save/Cancel, repeated stop/export/delete, playback failure/retry, and selected-session isolation.
- [ ] Check compact/standard phones, landscape, tablet/expanded width, and font scale 2.0. Confirm no clipped actions, nested transcript scrolling, color-only information, or unreachable controls. Test TalkBack cue explanations, speaker editing, tabs, playback, and unavailable states. The added narrow/large-font Compose test has only been compiled unless explicitly recorded as executed.
- [ ] Watch gate separately: pair a Galaxy Watch, explicitly opt in, obtain real immutable PPG batches, and verify independent Green/IR quality and ONNX provenance, canonical `[30s,270s)` finalization, baseline warm-up, transport-gap abstention, and per-channel markers. Watch disconnection must not stop local audio. Without this hardware evidence, PPG acceptance stays pending.
- [ ] Measure actual startup, transcription backlog/latency, memory, CPU, battery, and thermal behavior on the target device. No sleep-based synthetic benchmark is acceptance evidence.
- [ ] Confirm explicit deletion removes the selected encrypted artifacts and local playback cache without affecting other sessions. Remember an exported plaintext TXT is controlled by its external destination and is not deleted with the app session.

## Research decisions and limits

The implementation follows [Sherpa's native speaker diarization API](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/speaker-diarization/index.html) and [its Kotlin speaker-embedding API](https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/blob/v1.13.4/sherpa-onnx/kotlin-api/Speaker.kt). Stable identity is acoustic evidence, not a language-specific role classifier. Known diarization failure modes motivate explicit overlap/ambiguity abstention; bilingual accuracy still needs real recordings and speaker-error evaluation.

Live ownership follows [Compose UDF](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/architecture). Kotlin's [SharedFlow buffer contract](https://kotlinlang.org/api/kotlinx.coroutines/kotlinx-coroutines-core/kotlinx.coroutines.flow/-shared-flow/) documents that replay-zero flows do not retain emissions without subscribers. [UNDISPATCHED initialization](https://kotlinlang.org/api/kotlinx.coroutines/kotlinx-coroutines-core/kotlinx.coroutines/-coroutine-start/-u-n-d-i-s-p-a-t-c-h-e-d/) registers collectors before capture; serialization of snapshot assembly prevents caller-captured older state from replacing a newer publication. These are software guarantees tested with numeric/text fixtures, not measured microphone latency.

The [Indonesian pragmatic hedging study](https://www.ccsenet.org/journal/index.php/elt/article/view/0/40170) supports treating hedge expressions in context, not equating every marker with respondent deception. The project's lexicon is a transparent implementation heuristic, not a reproduction of that study's validation. Human-reviewed precision/recall, context expansion, and explicit user dismissal are appropriate subsequent work.

The native integration uses [SMILEapi 3.0.2](https://github.com/audeering/opensmile/blob/v3.0.2/progsrc/include/smileapi/SMILEapi.h) and unchanged [eGeMAPSv02 LLD definitions](https://github.com/audeering/opensmile/blob/v3.0.2/config/egemaps/v02/eGeMAPSv02_core.lld.conf.inc). The [GeMAPS publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAFFC.2015.2457417) describes acoustic descriptors; extracting them does not validate a stress, arousal, or deception decision. Five-utterance warm-up, pitch entry/exit 4/2 semitones, voiced persistence 1 second, and cooldown 10 seconds are review heuristics, not clinical thresholds. Speaker matching thresholds likewise remain uncalibrated on real bilingual interviews.

The [audEERING Research License](https://github.com/audeering/opensmile/blob/v3.0.2/LICENSE) restricts this integration to noncommercial research/teaching use. The APK includes its license and upstream notices. Commercial product use/distribution needs a separate license. No assertion of commercial release readiness is made.

Word-level timestamps, response latency relative to an identified question, semantic contradictions, a calibrated acoustic arousal model, automatic probing/haptics, and a fusion/deception score are not implemented or advertised. Replacing Sherpa/AudioRecord with Whisper/Oboe was not required to deliver the requested review workflow. Lean authoring guidance was applied by reusing existing runtime, consent, encryption, storage, and Watch gates and adding pure, separately tested cue/report/prosody modules.
